Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 · In-development version

Spenzia hasn't launched yet, so this policy covers two things: (1) this website, and (2) how the app is designed to handle data once it ships. We'll republish a finalized version, linked from the Play Store listing, before Spenzia goes live.

This website (spenzia.kyralabs.dev)

If you sign up to get notified, we collect only your email address, so we can send you one message when Spenzia is ready to download. We don't sell, rent, or share it, and we don't use it for anything else. Standard hosting logs (IP address, browser type) are kept by our host for security; we don't run our own analytics or ad trackers on this page.

Inside the Spenzia app

Spenzia is built around a local, on-device database, available across Web, Android, iOS, Linux, and Windows. By design:

  • Expenses, budgets, and categories you enter are stored on your device.
  • Spenzia does not require an account or a cloud sign-in to use its core features.
  • We do not include third-party advertising or data-broker SDKs.

If a future version adds optional features that involve data leaving the device (for example, cloud backup or bank statement import), this policy will be updated first, and that feature will be opt-in.

Permissions

Spenzia only requests the device permissions it needs for the feature you're using (for example, notification permission to remind you about a recurring bill). Requesting a permission for a feature you don't use is avoidable and we intend to avoid it.

Your choices

Since your expense data lives on your device, you control it directly — export or delete it from within the app once available, or uninstall the app to remove it entirely. For the notify-me email, write to kyra.labs.3011@gmail.com to have it removed at any time.

Children's privacy

Spenzia isn't directed at children and isn't designed to knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

We'll update the date above whenever this policy changes, and post a clear notice on the app's page for any material change.

Contact

Spenzia is built and maintained by Kyra Labs, an independent one-person studio. Reach out at kyra.labs.3011@gmail.com for anything privacy-related.
