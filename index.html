Categories & budgets

Set a budget once. Spenzia tracks the rest.

Group spending into categories that match how you actually live — food, rent, transport, whatever you need — and set a monthly limit for each. Spenzia keeps a running total so you always know how much room is left before payday.

Spending insights

See the pattern, not just the numbers.

A simple monthly view shows spending trends over time, and a category breakdown shows exactly what's driving the total — so "where did it all go" has an actual answer.

Recurring bills

Never get surprised by a bill again.

Mark a bill as recurring once — rent, subscriptions, utilities — and Spenzia reminds you before it's due, so it's already budgeted for instead of showing up as a surprise.
